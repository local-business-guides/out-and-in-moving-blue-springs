Start with the work your move requires

A mover near me search can turn up many choices, but the right fit depends on what needs to be moved and what help you want. Think beyond the number of boxes: a full household, an office, or a few heavy pieces each calls for a different plan.

Before you call, make a rough inventory and note your moving date, the places movers will need to enter, and any items that need extra care. That gives you a useful starting point for discussing the job.

What to prepare and what the service can cover

Moving help can include residential or office moves, packing and unpacking, loading and unloading, and furniture assembly or disassembly. The team also handles pianos, safes, and other heavy items. If you have one of these, bring it up early so it can be considered when planning the crew and equipment.

A short pre-call checklist can make planning easier:
- List the rooms and larger items being moved.
- Note stairs, narrow entries, or other access details you know about.
- Identify furniture that may need to come apart or be put together.
- Separate items you want packed from those you plan to pack yourself.
- Share your preferred date and any timing concerns.

One common mistake is waiting until moving day to mention a piano, safe, or difficult access. Another is packing everything before deciding whether you want help with packing or furniture disassembly. Call a professional when a heavy item needs specialty handling or you are unsure how to move it safely. Confirm which tasks you want included before the appointment.
