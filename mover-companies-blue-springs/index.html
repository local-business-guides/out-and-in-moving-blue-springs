A practical checklist for comparing service

Available moving services include residential and office moves, packing and unpacking, loading and unloading, furniture assembly and disassembly, and specialty handling for pianos, safes, and other heavy items. Ask about the portions of that list that apply to your move.

Use this checklist when you contact a mover:
- Write down the items or rooms included in the move.
- Share your desired date and any date constraints.
- Describe access at the starting and destination addresses.
- Point out heavy or delicate items that need special attention.
- Decide whether you want packing, unpacking, or furniture work.
