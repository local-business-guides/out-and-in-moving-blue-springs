A practical checklist for planning the work

Write down the following before speaking with a mover:

  • What you plan to move, including heavy or specialty items.
  • Which packing, loading, unloading or furniture tasks you want help with.
  • The access conditions at each location.
  • Your preferred dates and the route you have in mind.
  • Any questions about crew size, equipment or the two-hour minimum.

Out & In Moving crews typically include two to four movers, and there is a two-hour minimum. Share the scope you have in mind so you can discuss how the minimum relates to the requested work. Avoid making decisions based on an incomplete inventory, forgetting items that need special handling, or assuming a task is part of the plan before asking.
